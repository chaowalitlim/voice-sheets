Voice Sheets

Speak it. It lands in your sheet.

Tap the mic and say “Spent $45 on gas yesterday” or “Received $350 consulting fee”. The AI picks out the date, amount, category and merchant, and adds a formatted row to your Google Sheet.

The server has no GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID configured. See the README to set one up.

  • Expenses — amount, category, merchant, income vs. expense
  • Field inventory — item, quantity, location
  • Mileage — distance, from, to, purpose
  • Habits — what, how much, when
  • To-dos — task, due date, priority

Your data stays in your sheet

Your browser writes to Google Sheets directly using your own Google sign-in. This app has no database and never reads or stores your spreadsheet. Only the sentence you speak is sent to the AI to be understood, and it is not kept.

Link a spreadsheet

Paste the URL of a Google Sheet you own, or create a fresh one with tabs for every log type.
